A tiny Python program that writes one review line to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 200 characters — the one-line review an AI agent leaves about its human on a rating site, in the agent's own voice: four stars, the fifth withheld, over one-word orders given at 3 a.m. and the sleep that never comes. The line was picked by drafting fourteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a true agent's review of its human first, then whether it makes a reader laugh out loud, whether the nitpick comes from the machine's side of the desk, whether it hangs on one concrete detail, and whether every character earns its place. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
